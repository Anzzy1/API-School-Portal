About Aguinaldo Polytechnic Institute

Empowering the next generation of leaders and innovators

Our Mission

To provide accessible, quality education that develops students into competent professionals, critical thinkers, and responsible citizens who contribute meaningfully to society.

Our Vision

To be a leading educational institution recognized for academic excellence, innovative teaching, and producing graduates who excel in their chosen fields globally.

Core Values

  • Academic Excellence
  • Integrity & Honesty
  • Innovation & Creativity
  • Social Responsibility
  • Inclusivity & Diversity

Our History

Aguinaldo Polytechnic Institute was founded by Mohamad Anzar Pangcoga with a vision to provide accessible, quality education to students seeking to develop their skills and become competent professionals in their chosen fields.

Since its establishment, the institution has grown to offer a wide range of programs across multiple disciplines, including Business, Computer Science, Information Technology, Education, Criminology, Hospitality Management, Tourism Management, Computer Engineering, and Public Administration.

With a commitment to academic excellence and holistic student development, Aguinaldo Polytechnic Institute continues to empower the next generation of leaders and innovators who contribute meaningfully to society.

Frequently Asked Questions

What programs does Aguinaldo Polytechnic Institute offer?

We offer 12 programs: BSA, BSBA, BSCS, BSIT, BSIS, BELEMed, BSED, BSC, BSHM, BSTM, BSCpE, and BPA, each designed to provide industry-relevant skills and knowledge.

How do I enroll?

Click "Enroll Now" on the homepage, fill out the multi-step enrollment form with your personal and contact information, and submit. You'll receive a confirmation with your student ID.

What are the tuition fees?

Tuition varies by program. BSA: PHP 20,000/sem, BSBA: PHP 18,500/sem, BPA: PHP 18,500/sem, BSCS: PHP 22,000/sem, BSIT: PHP 22,000/sem, BSCpE: PHP 23,000/sem, BSIS: PHP 21,000/sem, BSC: PHP 20,000/sem, BSTM: PHP 20,000/sem, BSED: PHP 18,000/sem, BELEMed: PHP 18,000/sem, BSHM: PHP 21,000/sem. See our Tuition page for full details.

How do I access the student portal?

Use your registered email and the password you created during enrollment to log in. If you haven't enrolled yet, click "Enroll Now" to get started.

Are scholarships available?

Yes! We offer academic scholarships, merit-based grants, and financial assistance programs. Visit our Help Center for more information.

What are the admission requirements?

Requirements include: Form 138 (Report Card), PSA Birth Certificate, 2x2 ID photos, Good Moral Certificate, and a completed application form. International students may require additional documents.

Privacy Policy

Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you provide to us when you enroll, including your name, email address, contact details, academic records, and other information necessary for your education.

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used for enrollment processing, academic record keeping, communication regarding school matters, and improving our educational services.

Data Protection

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties except as required by law or with your explicit consent.

Your Rights

You have the right to access, update, or request deletion of your personal information. Contact our data protection officer at dpo@aguinaldo.edu.ph.
